FAQ

Quick answers.

Is it really free?

Yes. Free and open source. No account, no watermark, no ads in the app.

Is it always recording?

Only while you play. A short rolling buffer sits in memory. Nothing is saved until you press the key.

Will it cost me FPS?

Flash uses your GPU's hardware encoder, so the impact is small. Real numbers are coming.

Windows shows a blue warning.

The beta is not code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. Signing comes before 1.0.

Does Linux work?

Yes, as an experimental AppImage on Wayland or X11. Tell us what breaks.
